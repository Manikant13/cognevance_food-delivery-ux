# User Research

## Method
Survey and short interviews with [5-10] people who order food online.

## Participants
- Age range: [18-35]
- Type: [students, working professionals]

## Pain Points Found
| # | Pain Point | How Many Users |
|---|------------|----------------|
| 1 | [Slow checkout] | [4 of 6] |
| 2 | [Hidden delivery charges] | [3 of 6] |
| 3 | [Hard to find restaurants] | [3 of 6] |

## Key Insights
- [Users want fewer steps to order]
- [Users want clear pricing before payment]

## User Journey Map
| Stage | Action | Feeling | Opportunity |
|-------|--------|---------|-------------|
| Discover | Opens app, browses | Curious | Show popular items |
| Choose | Selects restaurant | Unsure | Add ratings and filters |
| Order | Adds to cart | Neutral | Simple cart |
| Pay | Checks out | Anxious | Clear price breakdown |
| Receive | Tracks order | Excited | Live tracking |
